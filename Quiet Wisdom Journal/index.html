How to Use This Journal

Privacy & How This Works

What you write stays on your device

Everything you type in this journal — your daily reflections, gratitude notes, and the Notes page — is saved only in your own browser, on your own device. It is never sent anywhere, never stored on a server, and nobody at Clarity Edge Counselling (or anyone else) can see it. There is no account, no database, and no way for us to read your entries even if we wanted to.

Because of this, your entries are tied to this device and this browser. If you clear your browser's site data, switch devices, or use a different browser, your entries won't carry over — there's no backup to restore from. If that matters to you, use Export My Journal from the contents screen any time to download a copy of everything you've written.

What we do collect

When you sign up, we collect your first name and email address so we can send you access to the journal and the occasional note from Clarity Edge Counselling. If you tell us this is for your team, we note that too, so we can tailor what we send. That's the full extent of what we collect through this journal.

This information is sent to Brevo, the email service we use to send journal access and updates. We don't sell or share it with anyone else. You can unsubscribe at any time from the link in any email we send, or email us directly to ask us to delete your details.

This isn't a monitored service

No one reads what you write here — not us, not anyone. This journal is a self-guided tool for private reflection, not a substitute for professional support, and it's separate from any clinical counselling relationship with Clarity Edge Counselling. If you're going through a difficult time and need to talk to someone, Lifeline is available 24/7 in Australia on 13 11 14, or reach out to your GP or a mental health professional.

Questions

If you have any questions about how this journal handles your information, you're welcome to get in touch via clarityedge.com.au.
